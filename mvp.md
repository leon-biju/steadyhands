# Steady Hands

A rage-inducing game where you must not let the reactor explode!

* Hand tracking is a must where you hold buttons and stuff
* Combine with the button presses part to prevent reactor meltdown

* The essence is the user must be overwhelmed by the game with too many things to do at once


Interface:
* A reactor control panel fills the screen
* Two floating hands (different colours) sit on top of it and follow your real hands
* Webcam window in the top right corner so you can see yourself
* Calibration at the start: show your right hand, then your left, then pinch with both
* Buttons need you to hold them (pinch, fist or both hands) for 3 seconds, then you get a message


Optional extras:
* A stupid local model that gives you really unhelpful suggestions
MSDa
* A mini-game when you have to get rid of Homer Simpson
* Messages that keep getting in your way and you have to pinch to close them
